About QED

Providing guidance and experience.

QED is small, woman-owned business specializing in program evaluation and research consulting. Our team has over 30 years of experience serving higher and K-12 education institutions, academic researchers, and grant-funded projects.

We guide both emerging and experienced researchers through the complexities of their research, ensuring their work meets the highest standards of quality, while staying on schedule, and within budget.

Led by Randi Sims, Ph.D., our business offers the expertise of a large consultancy with the responsiveness and personal care of a trusted colleague.
